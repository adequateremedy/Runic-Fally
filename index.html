Eidolon School

Class 1: Runic-Fally (Ages 2-3)

Welcome to Eidolon's School for the Gifted, little one.

I am sure you have already felt it—that very unique energy humming inside you. Even at such a young age, your magic follows you, making connections and growing right alongside you.

In Runic-Fally, we teach our youngest Fae how to guide that energy. Using just your fingertip, you will link matching Runic Stones together. As you draw your finger across them, the ancient symbols will glow white. Simply release your touch to collect the chain and earn points toward your grade!

In order to pass this class successfully, you must gather 2,000 points.

But be mindful, little one: every new round introduces a brand new Runic Symbol to the board. The more rounds it takes you, the harder it will become to see the connections. If the board becomes too difficult, you may be forced to restart your lesson from the beginning.

How to weave your magic:

  • You must connect 3 or more matching stones to form a chain. The larger the chain, the more points you collect!
  • 3 Stones = 1 pt
  • 4 Stones = 5 pts
  • 5 Stones = 10 pts
  • 6+ Stones = 20 pts

Star Magic:

Anytime you collect a chain of 4 or more, you will earn a Star. You may tap 'Place Star' and swap it with any stone on the board. It will stay there until you are ready to activate it. To activate it, simply tap it one more time to release a brilliant burst of magic!

Do not fret if you need to rest; your progress is safely anchored at the end of every round, allowing you to leave and return without losing your work.
