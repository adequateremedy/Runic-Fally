Eidolon School

Class 1: Runic-Fally (Ages 2-3)

Welcome to Eidolon's School for the Gifted, little one.

I am sure you have already felt it—that unique Energy inside you. Even at such a young age, that Energy follows you, making connections and growing right alongside you.

In Runic-Fally, I teach our youngest Fae how to guide that Energy. Using just that sparkle in your fingertip, you will link matching Runic Stones together. As you drag your finger across them, the ancient symbols will glow white. Release your touch, and collect the chain to earn points toward your grade!

In order to pass this class successfully, you must gather 2,000 points.

But be mindful, little one: every new round introduces a brand new Runic Symbol to the board. The more Rounds it takes you, the harder it will become to see the connections. If the board becomes too difficult, you may be forced to restart the class from the beginning.

Making Runic Stone Chains:

  • You must connect 3 or more matching stones to form a chain. The larger the chain, the more points you'll collect!
  • 3 Stones = 1 pt
  • 4 Stones = 5 pts
  • 5 Stones = 10 pts
  • 6+ Stones = 20 pts

Star's Energy:

Anytime you collect a chain of 4 or more, you will earn a Star. If you collect 3 Regular Stars, they automatically merge into a powerful Big Star! Collect 3 Big Stars, and they forge a massive Giant Star.

You may tap any of your Star buttons and swap it with any stone on the board. To activate it, simply tap it one more time to release a brilliant burst of Energy, destroying nearby Runic Stones for points!

Do not fret if you need to rest; your progress is safely anchored at the end of every Round, allowing you to leave and return without losing your place.
